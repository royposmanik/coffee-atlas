# Coffee Atlas

A single-page explorer of world coffee from farm to cup to bin: a rate-based cup clock, consumption and production since 1990, origins, demand by region and per person, a cherry-to-cup mass flow, waste streams and a personal footprint calculator.

Inspired by [Plastic Atlas](https://plastic-atlas.vercel.app/).

## Data

Figures are rounded values drawn from the International Coffee Organization, USDA FAS, Our World in Data (Poore & Nemecek 2018), the Water Footprint Network, Mussatto et al. (2011), the UK House of Commons EAC (2018) and Bunn et al. (2015). The 1990–2024 series is a rounded reconstruction; check the original releases before citing.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
